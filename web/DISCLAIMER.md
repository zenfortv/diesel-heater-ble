## 2. Danger
A diesel (or petrol) air heater burns fuel inside your vehicle, boat, tent or building. A fault in the heater,
its installation, its fuel or exhaust system, its electrical wiring, or in the way it is controlled can cause
**fire, explosion, carbon-monoxide poisoning, burns, damage to the battery and the electrical system, loss of the
vehicle and everything in it, serious injury and death**.

Remote control, timers and the "smart thermostat" make the heater start **automatically, without anyone
watching it**. That adds risk.

## 3. The Software can fail
The Software can and will fail in ways nobody foresaw, for example:
- the Bluetooth link drops, the phone is out of range, the battery is flat, or Android stops the app;
- commands are not received, arrive late, or the heater does something other than what was sent;
- the heater keeps running after the app has lost the connection: the app can then **not** switch it off;
- the temperature sensor reads wrong; the heater's clock, timers or settings differ from what the app shows;
- bugs, wrong protocol details for your model, updates of the phone, the browser or the heater.

The safety functions of the Software (minimum run and rest times, start limits, low-battery and fault lockouts,
alerts) are a convenience. They are **not** a safety device and must never be relied on to prevent a fire,
poisoning or any other damage.

- check that unattended and remote operation is allowed and safe where you use it, and not leave the heater
