Connect your heater

Switch the heater on and close the AirHeaterBLE app first: the heater accepts only one connection at a time.

Running the ESP32 box or the PC server? Open its address instead (for example http://heater.local): timers then work 24/7.

Terms of use and safety

Change heater password

The new password is set here, then confirmed on the heater's panel. If anything goes wrong, nothing changes on the heater.

Now on the heater's panel:

  1. Heater switched off (standby): open the engineering mode (gear + power for 3 s on most panels, or ▲ + ▼).
  2. Step to the Bluetooth password item (En09, or 010).
  3. Keep the gear button pressed until the number on the panel changes to .
  4. The panel shows SUC: done.
